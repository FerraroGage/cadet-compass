## Gaps and cautions
- ribbons: still not written. Ribbon order is only on a poster; the only text version is an editable .doc download (not downloaded) and CCR 672-5-1 covers senior ROTC only.
- chain: only the Cadet Command level (White House to USACC) is published as text; no unit-level cadet chain.
- The "Cadet Army JROTC Cadet Reference" handbook was not found on an official .mil site; knowledge is limited to the mission statement in CCR 145-2. Army values, creed and history were not sourced.
- The ranks table (Appendix E) is an image; insignia come from para 8-12b text. That text gives Cadet Private First Class as "one chevron above one bar"; this wording looks unusual and is set to confidence "verify" (shown with a warning, never quizzed) because no second official source confirms it.
- CCR 670-1 (uniform, 2007 revision) is older and was not used; the 2022 CCR 145-2 supersedes it for wear rules.
- TC 3-21.5 locators in chap 2 and 3 are chapter-level; paragraph numbers were not confirmed there.
- Drill is the Army manual; JROTC units may use it as cadet drill reference, but the regulation was not checked for any JROTC-specific drill supplement.
- All 5 chain facts set to verify by reviewer: the order was read from page tabs, not a stated chain of command. (13 Nov 2026)
