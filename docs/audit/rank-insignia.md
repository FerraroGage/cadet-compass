# Rank insignia pictures

Fetched from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-11-25, cropped and reduced for `public/img/ranks/`. The chart layout and short titles follow the Department of War rank insignia page (https://www.war.gov/Resources/Insignias/); that site refuses scripted downloads, so its own files are not used.

Changes: cadet enlisted pictures (E-2 to E-9) are the red and gold cloth artwork recolored to the black collar pin. Cadet Major, Lieutenant Colonel and Colonel diamonds are flat gray artwork given a silver sweep; Lieutenant Colonel and Colonel use the Army ROTC files for the same two and three diamonds.
