# Cadet Compass: brand info

**Name:** Cadet Compass
**Tagline ideas:** "Find your way through JROTC." / "Know it. Lead it."
**What it is:** A study app for learning JROTC knowledge (customs, drill, ranks, history, leadership).
**Logo idea:** A compass rose with red north. North is the direction cadets steer by, and the compass is the app's guide through the material.

## Font
Wordmark: Poppins Bold (free, SIL Open Font License). It is already converted to outlines in the SVGs, so you do not need to install it. Use Poppins or any clean sans-serif in the app UI.

## Files
- `vector/`: SVGs (scale to any size, edit in Figma, Inkscape or Illustrator)
  - cadet-compass-icon.svg: main round icon
  - cadet-compass-appicon.svg: rounded-square app icon
  - cadet-compass-icon-black.svg / -white.svg: one-color versions
  - cadet-compass-logo-horizontal.svg: icon + name (light backgrounds)
  - cadet-compass-logo-horizontal-dark.svg: icon + name (navy background)
- `png/icon/`: round icon, 16 to 2048 px (transparent)
- `png/appicon/`: rounded-square icon sizes for iOS/Android/web (120, 152, 167, 180, 192, 512, 1024)
- `png/horizontal/`: full logo, 400 to 3200 px wide, light and dark
- `png/mono/`: black and white icon, 1024 px
- `favicon.ico`: 16/32/48/64 px

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the center pin x 4.
- Do not stretch, recolor or rotate the compass.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the icon, 120 px wide for the full logo.
- This is an original design and does not use any official military emblem. Keep it that way, and do not add service seals or insignia without permission.
