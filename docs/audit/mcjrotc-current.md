# MCJROTC current-source findings (checked 12-13 Nov 2026)

Key limitation: the official site www.mcjrotc.marines.mil has been stripped down. Its site map now lists only Unit Home, About, Leadership Traits/Principles, Leaders and Biography. The old Regional Map, School List and Downloads pages return 404. No official page was found for questions 1 (chain), 2 and 3.

## 1. Chain of command
- MCJROTC publishes no chain of command on its current site: NOT FOUND. Looked at: /, /About/, /About/Leadership-Principles/, /Leaders/, /Unit-Home/Site-Map/, /General-Info/Downloads/ (404), and the 2002-era POI PDF (it has a "Chain of Command" skill but no names or current offices).
- Secretary of War: CONFIRMED. war.gov lists "Secretary of War HON Pete Hegseth" and "Deputy Secretary of War HON Steve Feinberg" (the page's own text still says "secretary of defense" in its description).
  - Quote: "Secretary of War HON Pete Hegseth Deputy Secretary of War HON Steve Feinberg"
  - URL: https://www.war.gov/About/Leadership/ (read live 12 Nov 2026)
  - Per the Sep 2025 executive order (secondary title only, per press reports, not verified at a .gov page), "Secretary of War" is an additional title for the Secretary of Defense. Statutory name unchanged unless Congress acted (not checked).
- Unofficial school pages (not .mil, treat as hints only): they list President, Secretary of War, Secretary of the Navy, Commandant (Eric M. Smith), CG Training and Education Command, Director MCJROTC, a Region Director, then school staff. One lists Thomas B. Savage as CG TECOM and Ryan A. Gnecco as Director MCJROTC. The official Leaders page contradicts that: Director MCJROTC is Col Robert G. Oltman USMC (Ret), Deputy Director LtCol Sean Charney USMC (Ret). So those school pages are unreliable.
  - Official Director page: https://www.mcjrotc.marines.mil/Leaders/ (undated; page text read 12 Nov 2026; footer 2026)
  - Quote: "Director MCJROTC COLONEL ROBERT G. OLTMAN USMC (RET)"
  - The official program order says MCJROTC is run under TECOM (MCO 1533.6E tags TECOM). Training Command and Marine Corps District: no evidence found in the chain. Region Director: exists per MCJROTC org (see Q2), but no official chain page found.

## 4. Leadership principle wording
- ANSWER: current wording is "Ensure" (not "Insure"), and the plural form "tasks".
  - Quote: "Ensure tasks are understood, supervised, and accomplished."
  - Source: MCWP 6-10 Leading Marines, dated 13 Aug 2024, status Current, PDF page 37, "Marine Corps Leadership Principles" list.
  - URL: https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/MCWP%206-10%20(SECURED).pdf
  - Listing: https://www.marines.mil/News/Publications/MCPEL/Electronic-Library-Display/Article/899578/mcwp-6-10/
- Position: it is listed 9th in the 2024 list, which opens with "Know yourself and seek self-improvement. Be technically and tactically proficient. Develop a sense of responsibility..." and has 11 principles total (verified by reading the page). It is not 6th. If the app numbers it 6, its list order differs from the current doctrine; re-check the app's whole list against the 2024 order: Know yourself; Be technically and tactically proficient; Develop a sense of responsibility among your subordinates; Make sound and timely decisions; Set the example; Know your Marines and look out for their welfare; Keep your Marines informed; Seek responsibility and take responsibility for your actions; Ensure tasks are understood, supervised, and accomplished; Train your Marines as a team; Employ your command in accordance with its capabilities.
- The MCJROTC site's own Leadership Principles page (https://www.mcjrotc.marines.mil/About/Leadership-Principles/) actually shows only Leadership Traits/core values, no principle list.
