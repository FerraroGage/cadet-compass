## Not written (no official source reached)
- ranks, ribbons, uniform, chain: the cadet guide / AFJROTCI 36-2001 / uniform and awards chapter is not on an official .mil page that could be opened. Only school-hosted copies turned up (varying editions), so nothing was written from them.
- regulations: promotion and grade requirements, for the same reason.
- AFMAN 36-2203 was not read; drill facts come from the AFJROTC drill guide instead. Not checked: whether AFJROTC drill differs from AFMAN 36-2203 in details.
- The fact sheet says "approximately 843 units"; unit and cadet counts were left out as they change.

## Second pass: ranks and ribbons (looser rule)
- ribbons: 62 facts (order 1-62), confidence medium. Source: AFJROTC/SFJROTC Operational Supplement Chapter 7, Uniform and Awards, 1 Aug 2023, para 7.6.3.2, copy at https://www.airforcewriter.com/Chapter7-Uniform-and-Awards.pdf. Only one copy of the 1 Aug 2023 edition was found. Items 1-62 were cross-checked against the 15 May 2022 edition (https://dunwoodyhs.dekalb.k12.ga.us/Downloads/7_Chapter_-_Uniform_and_Awards_-__15_May_2022.pdf) and the NC-075 unit guide of 17 Jul 2022 (http://ths.haywood.k12.nc.us/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2009/02/NC_075-AFJROTC-Cadet-Guide-17-JULY-2022-compressed-1.pdf); the order agrees in all three. Differences: the 2022 edition has a COVID-19 Ribbon at 63 where 2023 has a Resiliency Ribbon, and item 46 is named slightly differently. Item 63 and the five CAP awards (64-68) were left out. Strictly, the "two copies of the same edition" rule is not met for 2023. A 2024-25 or later edition may exist; none found.
- ranks: NOT written. The ranks list (Cadet Airman Basic through Cadet Colonel) appears only in unit-written handbooks (for example NC-074 SY25-26) and the Cypress Springs guide, none showing a Holm title, number and edition date; Chapter 7 shows insignia only as images, and DAFI 36-2010 (24 Jun 2025) has no grades.
- AFMAN 36-2203: not found. e-publishing.af.mil returned 404 for afmanl36-2203 and dafman36-2203; only base supplements show up. So no comparison with the drill guide was possible.

## Shared with ussf
- All 62 ribbons facts have branch ["usaf","ussf"]. Supporting text, Ops Sup Chapter 7 (1 Aug 2023): the foreword says the chapter describes how "Air Force Junior ROTC (AFJROTC) and Space Force Junior ROTC (SFJROTC) cadets" wear the uniform, rank, ribbons and medals; para 7.6.3.4 refers to "AFJROTC, SFJROTC and CAP Medals"; the 15 May 2022 edition (para 7.6.2.3-7.6.3.1) refers to the "AFJROTC and SFJROTC Ribbon Chart".
- Drill (37), knowledge (10) and regulations (3) stay ["usaf"]: the drill guide, af.mil fact sheet and Cadet Information page, as read, do not say they apply to Space Force JROTC. content/ussf/ not touched.

## Ranks (third pass)
- ranks: 15 facts, grade order only (Cadet Airman Basic E-1 to Cadet Colonel O-6), confidence medium, branch ["usaf"]. Primary source: NC-074 Cadet Handbook SY 2025-26 (pp. 68-69 grade tables, Chapter 5). Second source agreeing: Clover HS SC-951 Cadet Handbook 2024-25, https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/4571/chs/4852797/Cadet_Handbook_2024-2025_Final.pdf (promotion requirements list Airman Basic through Chief Master Sergeant and Second Lieutenant in order, then "First Lieutenant to Cadet Colonel"). Limits: the second handbook is school year 2024-25, not the current year, and does not name the middle officer grades; the officer middle grades rest on NC-074 plus the standard officer order. Utah Military Academy SY25 also agrees on permanent grades. No grade disagreements found. Insignia not written.
- Space Force sharing: no ranks source mentions Space Force JROTC, so ranks stay ["usaf"]. Ribbons were already set to ["usaf","ussf"] (see above).
