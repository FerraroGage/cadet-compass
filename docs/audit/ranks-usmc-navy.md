# Marine Corps and Navy ranks: sources and gaps (2026-11-25)

Added to content/usmc/ranks.json: 86 facts (ordered rank + pay grade per rank, plus two stated-relationship facts).

Sources
- U.S. Department of War (formerly DoD), "U.S. Military Rank Insignia", https://www.war.gov/Resources/Insignia/ (defense.gov redirects here). Gives every title and pay grade used, the Sergeant Major over Master Gunnery Sergeant statement, and Petty Officer Third Class as the first petty officer rank.
- MCO 1020.34H (1 May 2018), Figures 4-10 and 4-12 (pp. 1-94, 1-95): Marine enlisted and officer titles in order from the top. Used to cross-check order.

Gaps
- First Sergeant above Master Sergeant (both E-8): only the MCO figure listing order supports it; marked confidence "medium".
- NAVPERS 15665 and navy.mil were not read; Navy order rests on the DoD pay grade chart. Rear Admiral (Upper Half) O-8 and (Lower Half) O-7 follow that chart.
- Warrant officers, Fleet/Command Master Chief, and wartime-only Fleet Admiral not added.
- No insignia descriptions added.
